Services

Everything between the factory and your production line.

Use us for the full journey or just the steps you need. Either way, you get one accountable partner.

Supplier sourcing

We find manufacturers in China that match your technical specifications, volumes and budget, and vet them before you commit.

  • Supplier search and shortlisting
  • Factory background checks
  • Samples and prototypes
  • Alternative supplier options

Price negotiation

We negotiate directly with suppliers in their language to secure competitive pricing, payment terms and realistic lead times.

  • Unit price and volume discounts
  • Payment terms
  • Production lead times
  • Contract and order terms

Quality control

Inspections at the factory before shipment confirm your goods meet specification, so defects are caught before they cross the ocean.

  • Pre-production checks
  • In-line production inspections
  • Pre-shipment inspection
  • Photo and written reports

Freight & containers

We arrange the most efficient way to move your goods, from full containers to consolidated shipments, by sea or air.

  • FCL and LCL ocean freight
  • Air freight for urgent orders
  • Consolidation from multiple suppliers
  • Shipment tracking and updates

Customs & import

We coordinate documentation and customs clearance in Mexico so your goods enter the country smoothly and compliantly.

  • Import documentation
  • Tariff classification support
  • Customs broker coordination
  • Duties and taxes estimates

Final delivery

From port to plant: we manage inland transport so your components arrive where and when your production needs them.

  • Port to plant transport
  • Delivery scheduling
  • Proof of delivery
  • Ongoing reorder support
