About us

Connecting two manufacturing powerhouses.

Forexza exists to make sourcing from China simple, safe and cost-effective for manufacturers in Mexico.

Who we are

Your intermediary, your advocate

We act as the link between manufacturing clients in Mexico and the suppliers in China who produce the components they need. Instead of managing factories, freight forwarders, inspectors and customs brokers separately, our clients work with one team that coordinates it all.

We aren’t tied to one product or industry. Whether it’s electronic parts, raw materials, packaging or machined components, we find the right supplier, secure the right price and make sure it arrives as specified.

Our mission

To give every Mexican manufacturer access to global supply, without the complexity.

Large corporations have dedicated international procurement teams. We give companies of every size the same advantage.
